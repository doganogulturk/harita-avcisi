# Google Play mağaza metinleri

Play Console → **Büyüme → Mağaza varlığı → Ana mağaza girişi** ve **Sürümler**. Türkçe varsayılan dildir; İngilizce
(en-US) çeviri olarak eklenir. Uzunluk sınırları Play Console'unkiler: ad 30, kısa açıklama 80, tam açıklama 4000,
sürüm notları 500 karakter.

## İngilizce (en-US)

**App name**

```
Harita Avcısı: Map Quiz
```

**Short description**

```
Find provinces, countries, flags and capitals on the map. Race your friends!
```

**Full description**

```
How fast can you find a province, plate code, country, flag or capital on the map? Harita Avcısı (Map Hunter) is a map game with Türkiye's 81 provinces and 179 countries of the world, to play on your own or with friends.

ON MY OWN
• Race: 10 questions in 120 seconds. Your score goes on the leaderboard; on a tie the faster one wins.
• Practice: no timer, no sign-in. Play as long as you like and study one continent at a time.

WITH FRIENDS
• Duel: send a friend a code and answer the same questions at the same time.
• Room: play a few rounds with a group and see who leads the room.

FIVE MODES
• Türkiye: a province name appears, find it
• Plates: a licence plate code appears, find that province
• World: a country name appears, find it
• Flags: a flag appears, find that country
• Capitals: a capital appears, find its country

A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
The same 10 questions for everyone in each of the five modes, one try a day. See your place on today's leaderboard, keep your day streak going and share your result with 🟩🟥 squares.

BADGES AND PROFILE
17 badges such as day streaks, perfect rounds, all 81 provinces, 100 countries, duel wins, podium and summit. Your profile shows your race summary, duels and leaderboard positions.

A MAP THAT TEACHES
When you answer wrong, the place you clicked is named, and the map zooms in if the right place is small. After each round you get a question-by-question review.

FAIR LEADERBOARD
Race questions are picked on the server, and scores and times are verified there.

• English and Turkish
• Play in portrait or landscape; in landscape the map is twice as big
• Sign in with Google or as a guest; practice needs no account
• No ads, no tracking
```

## Ekran görüntüleri

Telefon için en az 2, en fazla 8 görüntü (9:16 ya da 16:9, kenar 320–3840 piksel). Önerilen sıra:

1. Ana ekran (Yarış ve Liderler panosu, Antrenman, Arkadaşlarımla)
2. Başkent sorusu (oyun ekranı, yatay)
3. Günün meydan okuması sayfası
4. Sonuç ekranı ve soru soru döküm
5. Düello (lobi ya da oyun)
6. Profil ve rozetler
7. Plaka ya da bayrak sorusu
8. Kurulum ekranı ve canlı önizleme

İngilizce sayfa için aynı ekranlar uygulama İngilizceyken çekilir.
